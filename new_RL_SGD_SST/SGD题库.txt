1.Field Trip 实地考察/实地考察旅行
参考笔记
Speaker 1
Initially hesitant due to part-time job shift.
Persuaded by social opportunities and unique learning experience.
Plans to try changing shift and save money to attend.
Speaker 2
Excited about the field trip to learn hotel management and business skills.
Highlights differences between seaside and city hotels.
Values social opportunities with other departments.
Speaker 3
Not planning to attend due to a football match.
Believes similar learning opportunities exist in city hotels.
零基础参考答案
The three speakers were talking about a school trip.
The first speaker talked about a part-time job and work time.
The first speaker also said something about meeting friends and new learning.
The second speaker talked about hotel work and business skills.
The second speaker also said something about sea hotels and meeting students.
The last speaker talked about a football match and not going.
The last speaker also said something about city hotels and learning experience.

2.Improve presentation skills
笔记参考
Speaker 1
Struggles with overreliance on notes and panic when speaking freely.
Plans to use keywords, record practice, and rehearse with peers.
Speaker 2
Recommends progressive practice: record first, then practice with friends.
Suggests using keywords instead of full sentences.
Emphasizes that mistakes are normal; focus on handling them well.
Speaker 3
Advises looking at objects at the back of the room to ease nerves.
Suggests preparing “rescue phrases” to recover from mistakes.
Stresses clarity of message over memorization.
零基础参考答案
The three speakers were talking about speaking practice.
The first speaker talked about using notes too much and feeling nervous.
The first speaker also said something about using keywords and practicing with others.
The second speaker talked about step by step practice and recording first.
The second speaker also said something about using keywords and accepting mistakes.
The last speaker talked about reducing nerves and staying calm.
The last speaker also said something about rescue phrases and clear message.
