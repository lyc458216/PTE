The water we use today has been around for a long long time. 
我们今天使用的水已经存在了非常非常久的时间。 
That is because water can recycle itself in the water cycle, which is also called the hydrologic cycle. 
这是因为水可以通过水循环（也称为水文循环）实现自我循环。
Let's start the cycle with the evaporation of water from the surface of the ocean. 
让我们从海洋表面的水分蒸发开始，启动这一循环过程。
As this moist air is lifted, it cools and water vapour condenses. 
当湿润的空气被抬升时，温度降低，水蒸气便凝结成小水滴。
It may form clouds. In fact, clouds are made up of tiny water droplets and ice crystals that are so small they can float in the air. 
这些水滴可能形成云。事实上，云是由极其微小的水滴和冰晶组成的，它们轻盈到可以在空中漂浮。 
This moisture in the atmosphere may be transported around the globe until it returns to the surface as precipitation. 
大气中的这种湿气可以被输送到全球各地，最终以降水的形式返回地表。
Precipitation may take the form of rain, snow, sleet or hail. 
降水可能表现为雨、雪、雨夹雪或冰雹。 
Now once this water reaches the ground, one or two processes may occur. 
当这些水到达地面后，可能会发生一两个过程。
Some of the water may evaporate back into the atmosphere, or the water may penetrate the surface and become groundwater. 
一部分水可能重新蒸发回到大气中，或者渗入地下，成为地下水。
The groundwater may take several different paths. 
地下水可能通过多种不同的路径流动。
The groundwater may seep in its way into the oceans river or streams, 
它可能缓慢渗入海洋、河流或溪流；  
or it may go into an aquifer, which is a body of saturated walk rock through which water can easily move, 
也可能进入含水层——即一种饱和的多孔岩石结构，水可以轻松穿过其中；
or it may be released back into the atmosphere through transpiration. 
还可能通过植物的蒸腾作用再次释放回大气中。
Transpiration occurs when leaves from trees and other vegetation give off water vapor through pores in their leaves. 
当树木和其他植物的叶片通过叶面的气孔释放水蒸气时，就会发生蒸腾作用。
Some water on the Earth's surface is not absorbed by plants or become ground water, and it becomes surface runoff. 
地球上部分地表水未被植物吸收或渗入地下成为地下水，而是形成地表径流。
This runoff may empty into lakes, rivers, and streams and then is carried back to the ocean where the cycle may begin.
这些径流可能流入湖泊、河流和溪流，最终汇入海洋，从而重新开始水循环。

56.national wealth and Adam Smith 国家财富与亚当斯密
答案:
A nation’s wealth was believed to be the money people could pile up. 
Then Adam Smith claimed that a nation's wealth came from agriculture as well as manufacture, and it was the ability to achieve high outputs. 
National wealth was equal to the nation's income, as it measured national output. 
As a result, his thought has set the stage for the era of industrialization. 
原⽂:
The concept of National Wealth has evolved significantly over time. 
Before the Industrial Revolution, British economists believed a nation's wealth lay in how much money people could pile up. 
This view focused on hoarding precious metals like gold and silver. 
However, this perspective was famously challenged by Adam Smith, who was a landowner and capitalist. 
In his 1776 book The Wealth of Nations, he claimed that a nation's wealth came from not only agriculture but also manufacture. 
For Smith, the nation's wealth was of the ability to achieve high outputs. 
In essence, overall, national wealth was equal to the nation's income since national income measured national output.
This thinking directly set the stage for the era of industrialization. 
Industrialization means harnessing machinery, factories, and new forms of energy to produce more output. 
Its goal is to fill the world with goods, precisely the goods you dress in, you eat—the tangible products that improve daily life. 
For decades following its rise, then manual manufacturing played the major role in this transformative process, moving production from homes to centralized factories.
Within this expanded view of wealth, agricultural outputs count, but they become one part of a larger economic engine. Adam Smith's framework teaches us that a nation's prosperity is dynamic.
