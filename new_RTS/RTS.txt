1. #49 Party
答案：
Hi! I would like to discuss an issue we have encountered with the food preparations for our upcoming party.
The background is that I'm planning to prepare barbecue and salad for everyone, but I don't know if you have any dietary restrictions.
In the situation, what I can think of is to ask you to text me your dietary needs.
Besides, another thing that comes to my mind is to prepare some vegetarian options.
原文：
You are going to have a party with your classmates at home. You will prepare barbecue and salad to treat them, but you do not know their dietary
restrictions. You made a phone call to ask them about this, but they did not answer the call. Please leave a message to them.

2. #81 New Classroom
答案：
Hi! I would like to discuss an issue we have encountered with the classroom change.
The background is that the computer broke down and the professor moved our class, but I don't know how to get to the new room and I'm already late.
In the situation, what I can think of is to ask you for directions to the new classroom.
Besides, another thing that comes to my mind is to meet you halfway so you can guide me.
原文：
When you arrive at the usual classroom where you normally have class, you find a notice saying that the computer is broken and you need to move to
another new classroom. However, you are late today, and the other students have already moved to the new classroom. So you ask a student about how to
get to the new classroom. What will you say?
